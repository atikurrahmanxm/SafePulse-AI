# SafePulse AI — Future Development Roadmap

## Phase 1 (Completed — Hackathon MVP)
- Bilingual (English & Bengali) React + FastAPI + SQLite/PostgreSQL SaaS platform
- TF-IDF + Logistic Regression phishing detector with held-out evaluation & phrase highlighting
- SSRF-safe URL & DNS (SPF/DMARC) security analyzer
- 10-domain CERT-In MSME aligned Business Security Health Check
- 7-scenario, 6-phase Interactive Incident Response Center
- Employee Cyber Awareness lessons, labeled simulations, and quizzes
- Branded ReportLab PDF exports and Demo Mode

## Phase 2 (Post-Hackathon Pilot with Guwahati SMEs)
- Expanded regional language support (Assamese & Hindi alongside English and Bengali)
- Multilingual fine-tuned transformer / Indic NLP phishing classifier trained on verified regional scam corpora
- Email header (`.eml` / SPF / DKIM / DMARC Alignment) parser for deeper BEC inspection
- Optional WhatsApp / Email forwarding bot integration with explicit opt-in consent

## Phase 3 (Commercial SaaS & Channel Partnerships)
- Multi-branch business management & role-based staff accounts
- Automated domain & SSL expiry / DMARC drift monitoring alerts
- Optional external reputation feed connectors (Google Safe Browsing, VirusTotal, PhishTank)
- Co-branded MSME cybersecurity packs for regional banks, pharmacy networks, and startup incubators
