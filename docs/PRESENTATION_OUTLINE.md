# SafePulse AI — Presentation Outline (AdtU Innovation Mela 2026)

1. **Title Slide**: SafePulse AI — *Smart Cybersecurity. Simple Protection. Safer Businesses.* (Assam down town University, Innovation Mela 2026).
2. **The Problem**: SMEs, pharmacies, and retail shops face rising WhatsApp/UPI scams, fake KYC links, and weak security hygiene without dedicated IT teams.
3. **Why Existing Enterprise Tools Fail SMEs**: Expensive SOC subscriptions, complex jargon, English-only alerts, and lack of actionable incident playbooks.
4. **Solution — SafePulse AI**: Unified SaaS platform combining AI phishing detection, SSRF-safe URL analysis, CERT-In MSME posture assessment, guided incident response, and employee awareness in English & Bengali.
5. **Technical Architecture**: React + TypeScript + Tailwind + Recharts frontend; FastAPI + SQLAlchemy (SQLite/PostgreSQL) + Argon2/JWT backend; Scikit-learn TF-IDF + Logistic Regression ML pipeline.
6. **AI/ML Transparency & Evaluation**: Real held-out test metrics (87.5% accuracy, 92.9% precision, 86.7% F1, 0.953 ROC-AUC), inline phrase explainability, and honest limitations.
7. **Security-First Engineering**: SSRF protection blocking localhost/private IPs, zero target URL execution, rate limiting, and per-business tenant isolation.
8. **Live Product Walkthrough**: Overview of the 4 SME demo scenarios + PDF export.
9. **Business Model & Commercial Potential**: Freemium tier for micro-shops; affordable monthly SaaS per business seat; regional language packs; white-label partnerships with MSME associations and cooperative banks.
10. **Roadmap & Q&A**: Immediate next milestones and open questions.
